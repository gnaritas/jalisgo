How it comes together

1Say burrito or tacos
that's your base — one or the other (or both, if you're serious)
2Point at your fillings
protein, rice, beans, fresh veggies — off the bar, in front of you
3It gets grilled fresh
made to order while you wait — nothing sitting under a lamp

Who it's for

Mall lunch, shopping-trip fuel, feeding the kids something they actually pick themselves: the bar keeps it simple. You can dial the veggies up or the beans down — it's your plate, built at the counter, priced at the counter (no published online price list yet).

Quick answers

What does “food-bar style” mean at JalisGo?

You order at the bar: pick burrito or taco, then choose the protein, rice, beans and veggies. It gets made fresh in front of you — owner Ricky Plata calls it “a fresh Mexican grill.” (Longview News-Journal, Oct. 26, 2026.)

Where is JalisGo in Longview?

In the food court of Longview Mall, 3500 McCann Rd, Longview, TX 75605 — Bombongo's treats counter is in the same food court.

What are your hours?

Monday–Friday 11 a.m.–8 p.m., Saturday 10 a.m.–8 p.m., Sunday 12–6 p.m.

Who owns JalisGo?

Ricky and Maria Plata — the family behind Bombongo, with locations in the Longview Mall food court, at 2502 Estes Parkway in Longview, and in Tyler.
