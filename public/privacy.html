Plain-words privacy notice

This is a small static site. It keeps things simple on purpose:

  • No cookies, no analytics, no tracking scripts. Nothing follows you from this site to anywhere else. There is not a single line of third-party JavaScript on these pages.
  • The contact form goes to a mailbox, not a database. What you write is delivered by email straight to the shop's inbox and used to answer your message — nothing else.
  • The map is Google's. The embedded map on the home and contact pages is served by Google; when it loads, Google receives standard browser data (like your IP address). See Google's privacy policy. The map only loads after you scroll to it, and you can open directions in your own maps app instead.
  • No accounts, no logins, no ads. This site sells nothing and stores nothing in your browser.

About this build

Business facts on these pages (opening coverage, hours, ownership, location) come from the Longview News-Journal's Business Beat column of Oct. 26, 2026. Anything the published coverage didn't state — phone number, prices, photos — is marked as “to be added” rather than guessed. Longview Mall and Bombongo names are used descriptively to describe where JalisGo is; this site is not published by or endorsed by Longview Mall.

Questions about these pages, or to correct anything on them: use the contact form.
