Our story · Longview, TX

Named for Jalisco.
Built in Longview.

JalisGo is a play on Jalisco — the Mexican state — by Ricky and Maria Plata, the couple already running Bombongo treat shops in Longview and Tyler. In October 2026 they added a fresh Mexican grill to the Longview Mall food court.

The grill

“It's a fresh Mexican grill,” Ricky Plata told the Longview News-Journal when the stall soft-opened on Oct. 26, 2026. The format is a food bar — burritos, tacos, bowls, quesadillas and nachos, each built with the protein, rice, beans and veggies the customer chooses.

“It's a fresh Mexican grill.”— Ricky Plata, owner (Longview News-Journal, Oct. 26, 2026)

The family behind it

Ricky and Maria Plata already run Bombongo — an ice-cream-and-snacks shop with two Longview locations (the mall food court and 2502 Estes Parkway) plus a third in Tyler. “Bombongo” is Spanish for marshmallow, and the counter is known for milkshakes, strawberries with cream, spicy fruit cups, corn on the cob and taco cups. JalisGo puts the family's grill in the same food court as their treats.
