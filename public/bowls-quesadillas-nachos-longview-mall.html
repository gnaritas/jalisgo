The three boards

Bowls

All the bar fixings — your protein, rice, beans and veggies — in a bowl instead of a tortilla. Easiest way to go heavier on the fresh stuff.

Quesadillas

Your fillings, grilled fresh. The board for people who want the cheese-and-grill version of the bar.

Nachos

Fresh-built nachos loaded with what you choose — the bar's sharing plate.

Prices: at the counter. No published online price list yet.

Quick answers

When did JalisGo open at Longview Mall?

Soft-opened 11 a.m.–2 p.m. on Oct. 26, 2026; regular hours since Oct. 27 — Mon–Fri 11–8, Sat 10–8, Sun 12–6. (Longview News-Journal, Oct. 26, 2026.)

Where in the mall is JalisGo?

Longview Mall's food court, 3500 McCann Rd, Longview, TX 75605 — park at the mall and walk in.

Are the quesadillas and nachos made to order?

Everything on the bar is built when you order it, with the protein, rice, beans and veggies you pick.

Do JalisGo's bowls include a tortilla?

A bowl is the bar's fixings served without the tortilla base — same grill, same fillings, different plate.
