Built fresh, your way

JalisGo — a play on Jalisco, the Mexican state — is a food-bar grill: point to what you want, watch it get made. Choose your protein, rice, beans and veggies; we build it hot in front of you.

1. Pick your base
burrito · taco · bowl · quesadilla · nachos
2. Pick your fillings
proteins, rice, beans, fresh veggies
3. We fire it
grilled fresh, made to order
