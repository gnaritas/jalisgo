Everything people ask

Where is JalisGo?

In the food court of Longview Mall, 3500 McCann Rd, Longview, TX 75605. Park at the mall — the food court is inside.

What are your hours?

Monday–Friday 11 a.m.–8 p.m., Saturday 10 a.m.–8 p.m., Sunday 12–6 p.m.

How does the food bar work?

Choose your base — burrito, taco, bowl, quesadilla or nachos — then choose the protein, rice, beans and veggies. It gets made fresh in front of you.

How much does food cost?

Prices are at the counter. JalisGo hasn't published an online price list yet; this answer updates when one exists.

Who owns JalisGo?

Ricky and Maria Plata — who also own Bombongo in the mall food court, at 2502 Estes Parkway in Longview, and in Tyler.

What is Bombongo?

The family's ice-cream-and-snacks shop — “bombongo” is Spanish for marshmallow. Milkshakes, strawberries with cream, spicy fruit cups, corn on the cob, taco cups and more, in the same food court.

Still curious?

Send a note — the contact form reaches the shop directly while its phone line is being set up.
